Sacred Customs

Odia Bridal Traditions

Nirbandha

The formal alliance solemnized with the offering of betel nuts and coconuts before Lord Jagannath.

Baran & Aarti

A royal reception as the bride's mother welcomes the groom with sacred aarti and tilak at the mandap threshold.

Hastaganthi

The sacred tying of the sacred matrimonial knot uniting two souls through Vedic chants and elders' blessings.

Saptapadi

Seven sacred steps around the holy fire pledging mutual devotion, nourishment, and perpetual companionship.

Sala Bidha

A playful custom where the bride's brother gently taps the groom, establishing joyful brother-in-law camaraderie.

Gruhaprabesa

The bride's auspicious entry into her matrimonial abode, ushering in peace and wealth with the gentle nudge of a rice pot.
