Sacred Customs

Odia Bridal Traditions

Nirbandha

The formal commitment ceremony where the families exchange auspicious betel nuts (Gua) and coconuts, officially formalizing the alliance.

Mangan

The sacred pre-wedding ceremony where seven married women apply turmeric paste and oil, blessing the bride with a radiant glow.

Diya Mangula Alati

A spiritual temple ritual where the bride's attire and bangles are blessed by the deity to ensure a smooth, protected ceremony.

Kanya Daan

The emotional Vedic ritual where the bride's parents formally hand over their daughter's hand to the groom, symbolizing her transition.

Hastaganti

The holy knotting ceremony where the couple's hands are bound with a sacred thread, betel leaves, and mango twigs over the fire.

Kaunri Khela

A lighthearted post-wedding game where the couple tries to open each other's closed fists to find hidden sea shells or coins.
